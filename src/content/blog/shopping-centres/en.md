Help visitors find shops, discover offers, and see what’s happening throughout your shopping centre. Digital totems and interactive displays put useful information where people need it, while giving stores and brands space to promote their latest news.

## Help visitors find their way

Make it easier to locate shops, restaurants, and facilities without searching for an information desk. Place digital directories at entrances, near escalators, and where walkways meet to help visitors decide where to go next.

Interactive displays let visitors browse the directory and explore a map of the centre at their own pace. The interface can be tailored to your layout, with clear categories for shopping, dining, entertainment, and services.

## Give offers and events more visibility

Use screens throughout the centre to highlight store promotions, new openings, seasonal campaigns, and upcoming events. Totems in busy areas give these messages a visible place along the visitor’s route.

Schedule content around campaign dates and opening hours, and choose where each message appears. A restaurant offer can run near the food court, while centre-wide events can be promoted across multiple displays.

## Keep information up to date

Store locations, opening hours, and visitor notices can change. Digital displays make it easier to update this information without replacing printed signs each time.

Share practical details such as holiday opening hours, temporary closures, and upcoming activities, so visitors can plan their visit with fewer interruptions.

## Manage screen content with NeoSlide

NeoSlide lets your team organise, schedule, and publish digital signage content from one cloud dashboard. Send updates to individual screens or groups of displays, depending on where the content is relevant.

Prepare campaigns in advance and keep everyday announcements consistent throughout the centre. Your team can manage what appears on each screen without updating displays individually on site.

## Plan the right setup for your centre

Totems work well for visible promotions and visitor information, while interactive displays let people search and explore for themselves. BlackBox can help you choose the right combination for your space and the way visitors move through it.
