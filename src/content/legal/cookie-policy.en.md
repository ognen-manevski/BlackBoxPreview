## 1. About This Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how BlackBox Solutions uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/).

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored on or accessed from your device when you visit a website.

Some cookies are necessary for a website to function correctly. Other technologies may be used for optional purposes such as analytics, enhanced functionality or marketing.

## 3. How We Use Cookies

We organise cookies and similar technologies into the following categories:

### Necessary

Required for essential website functionality, security and remembering your cookie preferences. Necessary technologies cannot be disabled through our cookie preferences tool.

### Analytics

Used, where enabled, to help us understand how visitors interact with the website and to improve its performance and content.

### Functional

Used, where enabled, to provide optional functionality or third-party services.

### Marketing

Used, where enabled, for advertising, campaign measurement or similar marketing purposes.

Optional categories are disabled by default and are activated only according to your preferences where consent is required.
